Vehicle Management Policy

General /Purpose

Vehicles are assets of an organization that are used for the smooth operation and function of programs and
activities. It is very important that vehicle maintenance and administration be properly done by the Mukti
Cox’s Bazar/organization management.

Use of Vehicle

Mukti Vehicles shall be used for official purposes only by following the procedure of Mukti Cox’s Bazar
Logistic Department and this Vehicle use guideline. Unless otherwise approved in writing by the Admin
Department (authorized person) use of any Mukti vehicle is strictly prohibited. Mukti vehicles shall never
be allowed to drive by unauthorized person(s) and person(s) having no valid driving license.

Vehicle use guideline

Day to day vehicle operation, movement its management and control by Admin department and logistic
department will be ensured maintenance, update BRTA other relevant papers. Any employee of Mukti
Cox’s Bazar could be raise vehicle request through Vehicle Requisition From (VRF) at least 3 working
days in advance and submit to admin department for approval. Upon receipt approval, logistic department
will assign only Mukti Cox’s bazar vehicle as appropriate. In case of unavailable of organizational vehicle
admin department will arrange rented car from Vendor for daily use.

If required or In case of Project staff transport facilities for their daily pick-up and drop-up from their
residence to duty station. Project office could be hired rent vehicle locally under full control of Mukti Cox’s
Bazar Admin department (such as ensure all the legal and precautionary procedure have been taken, all
safety and security, gender sensitivity and others precautionary measure sets by the organization) have
been applied and ensured.

For any unavoidable circumstances, as like as Political parties road block, strike or cease by the enforcement
agency, to cover up this situation guided by the security focal, Mukti Cox’s bazar vehicle is fully restricted
to movement.

Use of Car and Micro Bus ‘ JN Fea

a) The Chief Executive, Deputy Chief Executive, Coordinators and members of Executive Committee can
use car for Mukti official purpose by following the procedure of Mukti Cox’s Bazar Logistic Department
and Vehicle use guideline. Other H/O staff may travel official vehicle for assigned specific task by the
written approval from Admin Department (authorized person).

b) All other project staff can use vehicle or project vehicle for official purpose subject to project fund
availability and approval from administration.

Logistics Department bears the primary responsibility to comply all the formalities concerning vehicle
registration, renewal of all related papers and documents, road tax, fitness certificate for all Mukti Cox’s
Bazar vehicles. Registration of vehicles must be in the name of Mukti Cox’s Bazar. The original registration
book, fitness certificate, tax token and insurance policy papers shall be kept in the vehicle under the custody
of the driver, a photocopy of which shall be kept by the Logistic Department.

Vehicle Insurance Eines Uan men eit ane any

All vehicles of Mukti must be adequately covered by Insurance policies from a reputable Bangladeshi
Insurance company. Logistics Department will ensure availability of insurance policy for all the vehicles.
The competent authority will decide whether comprehensive insurance or first party or third-party insurance
coverage will be taken for the vehicle.

While taking the coverage, reputation of the insurance company in terms of settlement of claims must be
considered. Insurance coverage should be comprehensive and broad-based covering all probable risks. The
vehicles must not be under insured to avoid average settlement at the time of actual loss.

Drivers are responsible for notifying the Logistic Department when insurance policies and other documents
are to be renewed. This must be done in writing at least one month before the renewal is due. Logistic
Department should initiate claim if there is any damage of Mukti vehicles.

Maintenance of Log Book and Vehicle Movement Registe si

The driver(s) or person(s) authorized to drive the Mukti Cox’s Bazar vehicles are supposed to maintain Log
Book for the vehicles that they are driving. They shall write the log book properly filled up immediately
after ending the journey/trip. Whenever the vehicle is used to transport a passenger, the passenger is
required to initial the log book in the space designated therein. The log book must be reviewed regularly
and signed by the staff member in-charge of transport. The original log book must be kept on record for a
minimum of five years from the date of completion for the purpose of internal control and audit. However,
the period may be varied for specific project as per donor requirement. Drivers are fully responsible to
maintenance of Log Book and Movement Register.

Vehicle Safety and Security a eee cn aie

In order to ensure that the use of vehicles is in the best interests of Mukti, the following rules shall be
followed for safety and security:

+ Vehicles must be kept in good working condition in order to minimize maintenance cost and to
maximize its useful life

+ Only authorized person(s) holding a valid driving license will drive the vehicle. Person(s) driving
the vehicle must be physically and mentally fit to operate/drive a vehicle

+ No alcoholic beverages or other intoxicants should be consumed before or while driving the Mukti
vehicle. Persons authorized to use vehicles is required to report the use of any drugs or medication
(if suggested by the doctors) that might impair their ability to drive to the Administration
Department / immediate supervisor.

Servicing of Vehicles

Proper care must be taken for maintenance of the vehicle. Logistics Department will take necessary steps
for servicing all vehicles timely and shall send vehicles to the nominated service station after a regular
| interval or run of certain (3000-3500) kilometers.

| Repairing and Purchase of Spare Parts

The driver should fill in a (Vehicle Repair Request VRR) Form if repairing or replacement of any spares
is needed. VRR should be submitted to the vehicle in-charge or Administration Department for necessary
approval. Designated official will check whether requested repair / replacements are really needed for the
vehicle.

| If any item or part is found missing or damaged, the Transport In-charge or Administration Department
will assess and determine whether any reimbursement is to be claimed from the driver/concerned employee.

| Loss of Documents

If the Registration Book or any important document is lost, the driver must report it to the nearest police
station immediately and register a General Diary (GD). The police station provides acknowledgement of
the GD. The Driver then shall inform his supervisor and Mukti Office and provide a copy of the GD paper.
Administration Department will make a petition to the Bangladesh Road Transport Authority (BRTA)
requesting to issue a duplicate Registration Book along with the GD papers.
