Note for file
Date: 21 January 2026
Subject: Justification for expedited recruitment for the vacant position of SPO, MEP Program.
It is noted that Mr. Sanjoy Baidya, who was employed as Senior Program Officer (SPO) under the MEP Program, was terminated on 15 October 2025 due to breach of the HR Policy.
Subsequently, based on his written application and the decision of the Executive Committee (EC) Meeting, he was temporarily assigned duties. However, due to repeated violations of HR Policy, involvement in gross misconduct, and breach of organizational rules, a dismissal letter was later issued to him.
At present, the position of Senior Program Officer is vacant. Due to the vacancy, there is a risk of disruption to the smooth implementation of program activities. Considering the time-sensitive nature of the program and the need to ensure uninterrupted and timely implementation of program activities, it is proposed that recruitment to be completed through an expedited recruitment process by shortlisting candidates from the CVs already received under the previously published circular dated 10 July 2025 for the Senior Program Officer position.
This approach will save time and facilitate the continuation of program activities without further delay.
