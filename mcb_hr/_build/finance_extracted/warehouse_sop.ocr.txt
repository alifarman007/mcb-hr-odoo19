The Goods Receiving Note (GRN) is a standard Mukti Cox’s Bazar document to confirm
receipt of goods. It is the base document for financial transactions, such as the payment of the
supplier/forwarder and the recording of the accounting entries.

Bin/Stack Cards are standard Mukti Cox’s Bazar documents that are fitted to an item
bin/stack in the warehouse. The card displays the beginning balance, movements in and out
and the current balance of an item at any given time. One bin/stack should only comprise one
type of item from one unique Purchase Order (PO) number. The bin/stack cards facilitate
picking and physical counts.

The Bin/Stack Cards must be established or updated as a soon as an item is moved in or out
for a specific bin/stack. The warehouse staff effecting the movement must note the approved
Material Stock Request (MSR) number and sign after having updated the card.

The Stock Card is a standard Mukti Cox’s Bazar document which consolidates the
information of multiple bin/stack cards relating to the same item and Purchase Order (PO) in
case it is stored in different bins/stacks of the warehouse. It should be continuously updated by
the warehouse and maintained in the warehouse area.

Stock Card ys. Bin/Stack Card

In smaller warehouses, the Stock Cards provide the same information as the Bin/Stack Card. In
larger warehouses the Stock Card information will consolidate the information of multiple
Bin/Stack cards and show the total inventory holding of an item/ Purchase Order PO which is
stored in different bins/stacks.

The Stock Report is a non-standard Mukti Co’s Bazar document used to report the inventory
position/holding for all inventory items by Purchase Order PO, at warehouse level. The
reporting frequency will be determined based on the inventory turnover rate and/or the
fluctuation in demand but is normally monthly. It is the responsibility of the head office to
define the format of the report, and the reporting f-equency.

The report is used by the warehouse and the head office to report the inventory holdings at
warehouse and/or at filed level to stock owners and other donors. The reports provide timely
information on inventory levels by warehouse and item which is important for operational,
financial and procurement planning.

Quarterly Activities

Quarterly Inventory Verification: The Store keeper /officer in charge for inventories in
coordination with the Project warehouse focal point and guidance from Coordinator Finance &
Accounts must conduct a full Physical Inventory Verification quarterly. Any discrepancy
between the physical inventory as per count sheets and the Automation Inventory software stock
records must be investigated and explained by the Store Keeper of head office and the Project
warehouse focal. The head office must send the full documentation of the stock count including
the reconciliation reports with full explanation for all discrepancies to Senior Management. If
there is a high inventory turnover in the operation, the implementation of more frequent
inventory verifications is recommended.

Yearly Activities

Yearly Inventory Verifications: The Yearly Physical Verification of Inventories is jointly
performed by Mukti Cox’s Bazar head office core staff (Supply and Programme) and the Project
warehouse Focal person in accordance with instructions issued by the Coordinator Finance &
Accounts. As a part of this exercise, a full reconciliation between inventory quantities
established by physical counts and Automation Inventory software records needs to be
completed. Any discrepancy between the physical inventory as per count sheets and the
Automation Inventory software stock records must be investigated and explained by the head
office assign/designated and Project the warehouse and logistic Officer. The head office must
send the full documentation of the stock count including the reconciliation reports with full
explanation for all discrepancies to Senior Management.

Receiving Consignments

MCB Warehouses should only store inventories owned by MCB, and all consignments should have an
MCB PO number reference (this is also applicable for donations. MCB should be clearly stated as the
consignee on the Challan/Waybill (Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, Delivery Note from manufacturer, or
other official documents. The responsible officer in the MCB head office must ensure that all relevant
delivery information is provided to the warehouse well before the actualdelivery.

Objectives: The Logistic Officer is responsible for the physical receipt of goods, confirming that the
quantity and quality of the goods received is according to the specifications. In addition, s/he needs to
verify if the necessary documents received are in order and inform the MCB head office after completion
of receipt. An effective receiving process ensures that goods are properly inspected and received, stacked
away and stored, that inventory records are timely updated and the required documentation sent to the
MCB head office for update at Automation Inventory software.

Within the goods receiving area

Firstly, execute a visual inspection of the truck(s). Check if the goods are visibly in accordance with the
Challan and Invoice. Inspect the goods for obvious visible damages or loss (e.g., bad quality canvas on
the truck, holes in the container, destroyed outer packing, etc.).
Offloading & inspecting for damage or loss: Goods should be carefully offloaded and handled to
minimize damage following these general handling guidelines, if possible:

* Avoid outside offloading during rain;

“ Use forklifts for large or heavy items;

“+ Use trolleys for lighter ones;

* Do not drag, drop or throw items.
