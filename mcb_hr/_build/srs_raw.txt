[Heading 1] 1. Introduction

[Heading 2] 1.1 Purpose
[Normal] This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) defines the functional and non-functional requirements for implementing Odoo Enterprise as the ERP system for Mukti Cox’s Bazar (MCB). It covers the full HR lifecycle — employee records, recruitment, payroll, leave, training, expenses, and offboarding — aligned to MCB’s Human Resources Policy (July 2025).
[Normal] 
[Heading 2] 1.2 Organization Background
[Normal] Mukti Cox’s Bazar is a registered NGO based in Cox’s Bazar, Bangladesh, implementing humanitarian and development projects funded by UNFPA, CARE, UNICEF, and DFAT. MCB employs permanent, project-based, and contractual staff across multiple donor programmes, requiring a robust, donor-compliant HR and financial system.

[List Paragraph] MCB will obtain an Odoo Enterprise license with sufficient named user seats.

[List Paragraph] HR Policy (July 2025) is the authoritative policy; amendments during implementation require SRS revision.
[List Paragraph] Uploaded recruitment documents (Admit Cards, Marking Sheets, Expenditure PDFs) serve as output templates.
[List Paragraph] Reliable internet connectivity available at MCB Head Office for cloud Odoo access.
